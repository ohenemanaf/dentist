Questions

Good to know

Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are best, so you are not kept waiting. For emergencies, call and we will fit you in the same day.

Does treatment hurt?

We use local anaesthetic for anything that could be uncomfortable, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Do you accept health insurance?

We accept selected insurance schemes. Send us the name of your scheme on WhatsApp and we will confirm before your visit.

From what age can children come?

From their first tooth. An early, relaxed visit makes later visits much easier.

Can I pay in instalments?

For larger treatments such as braces and crowns, we can agree a payment plan with you.

Need a dentist today?

Toothache or a broken tooth? Call us. We keep same-day slots for emergencies.
