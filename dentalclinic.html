Types of Teeth We Care For

Understanding the different types of teeth and their functions helps in maintaining optimal oral health. We provide specialized care for every tooth in your smile.

Incisors

The sharp, chisel-shaped front teeth used for cutting food.

Canines

Pointed teeth located next to incisors, designed for tearing food.

Premolars

Teeth with a flat biting surface, ideal for crushing and tearing.

Molars

The largest teeth at the back of the mouth, used for grinding food.
